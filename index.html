03 / ADDITIONAL CHARACTERISTICS

What about Section 9.2?

Section 9.2 is relevance-based, not an automatic 18-item checklist. The properties below may need to be included when they are important for safe use. Physical state alone does not confirm or exclude these hazards.

9.2.1 — Physical hazard classes 17 categories
9.2.2 — Other safety characteristics 12 characteristics

For example, 9.1(g) explosion limits do not apply to solids, but 9.2.2(c) explosible dust/air mixtures may still matter for combustible powders.

Regulatory basis

This tool reflects the physical-state rules in REACH Annex II §9.1 and the relevance-based requirements in §9.2. It assists SDS authors; it is not a substitute for a qualified assessment of the actual product, available studies, CLP classification or applicable obligations.
